Your lunar calendar.
On your Home Screen.

Suili brings the Chinese lunar calendar, month widgets and important dates to your iPhone.

A whole lunar month

See Gregorian and lunar dates together. Browse months directly in the large Home Screen widget.

Your important days

Birthdays, anniversaries, Lunar New Year and days off. Choose up to four countdowns for one widget, and select a date for each Lock Screen widget.

Free everyday essentials

Calendars, all widgets, repeat rules and local reminders are free, with three custom dates.

One purchase. More room.

Suili Pro unlocks unlimited custom dates and removes banner ads. No subscription. Optional rewarded access lasts 24 hours on this device when available.

Includes the labelled 2026 mainland China holiday schedule; it is not a local overseas work schedule. Days off follow the mainland adjusted schedule; missing-year estimates are marked ≈. Dates stay local with no app account or iCloud multi-device sync.

农历和期待，
就在桌面。

岁历，将农历月历、桌面小组件和重要日子，放进同一个 iPhone App。

整个月，一眼看全

公历与农历同屏。大号主屏幕小组件轻点箭头切月，不必打开 App。

重要日子，放在一起

生日、纪念日、春节和休息日。一个小组件最多展示 4 个倒计时，锁屏也能选择关注的日子。

每天需要的，免费用

日历、所有小组件、重复规则与本地提醒免费，可保存 3 个自定义日子。

一次买断，更多空间

岁历 Pro 解锁无限日子并去除横幅广告，无订阅。广告可用时，可自愿观看视频获得本机 24 小时 Pro 体验。

包含明确标注的 2026 年中国大陆节假日安排，不代表海外当地工作日历。休息日计入调休；缺少年份安排时，≈ 表示按周六、周日估算。日子本地保存，无需 App 账号，当前不提供 iCloud 多设备同步。
