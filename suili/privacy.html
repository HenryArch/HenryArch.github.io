Suili · Privacy policy

Updated: September 20, 2026

Your important dates stay on this device, shared only with Suili widgets. We do not operate an account or upload your dates. Notifications are optional. Purchases are handled by Apple. When advertising is enabled, Google may process device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics under its policies. Important-date names and dates are never passed to the advertising SDK. Privacy choices are available in Settings where required. Pro removes ads. Removing the app deletes local data subject to your iOS backup settings. Rewarded video is optional. After the SDK reports completion, we store a local random reward identifier and its grant/expiry times for a 24-hour Pro experience. This record is excluded from device backup; it is not a purchase and cannot be restored with Restore purchases. Support email is handled only to respond to your request.

Google may process IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device/app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information when its services are used, under its privacy policy. Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is processed. Where required, a consent form is shown before ads; applicable choices remain accessible in Settings. You can change notification permission in iPhone Settings and delete individual saved dates in the app. Suili does not operate an account system or a server holding your dates. Apple handles purchase and payment information; Suili receives verified transaction status. Local reward access is not synced across devices. Support email can contain the information you choose to send; avoid including private events. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence where applicable.
