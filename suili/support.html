Reminders and saved dates

Enable a 9 AM reminder on an important date and allow notifications. Simple rules repeat automatically. Lunar dates, month ends and other complex rules are scheduled ahead, with up to 60 pending requests across the app. Check Settings → Reminders for coverage and reopen before it ends to refill. Reopen after changing time zones. Focus and Scheduled Summary can affect when alerts appear.

Dates are stored locally, without iCloud multi-device sync. Reinstalling after deleting the app does not guarantee data recovery; system backups depend on your iOS settings. Requires iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Alarms and adjusted workdays

On iOS 26 or later, system alarms support one-time alerts and chosen weekdays. Mainland China workday and day-off modes include holiday adjustments and always use Beijing time. The list shows the last date actually scheduled in the system; reopen Suili before then to extend coverage. The current adjusted schedule covers 2026. Unknown years are not estimated for alarms. Uses the default system sound; snooze and custom sounds are not included in this version.
