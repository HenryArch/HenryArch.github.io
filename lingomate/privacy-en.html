LingoMate is operated by FUNCONNECT PTE. LTD. and its affiliates (“we”). This policy explains how we process personal information when you use English learning, AI conversation, voice, lessons, memory, and notification features.

Human audio, chat content, lesson dialogue, and relationship memory derived from them may include sensitive or private information. Do not enter identity documents, financial accounts, precise addresses, medical records, or other people’s secrets that are unrelated to learning. AI output can be inaccurate and is not medical, legal, investment, or other professional advice.

1.1 Device permissions

  • Microphone: requested only when you actively use voice input, lesson recording, or a realtime call. Refusing still allows features that do not need voice. We do not extract voiceprint identity.
  • Notifications: requested only when you turn them on. You can disable them in the app or system settings; that does not affect ordinary chat and lessons.
  • App Tracking: requested only when ad attribution or Facebook is configured and the system has not yet chosen. After authorization, related SDKs may use the advertising identifier. After refusal they do not read it, and chat, lessons, and user features still work. Refusal does not turn off all analytics; SDKs may still use install, app, and device information that does not need that permission.

The current app does not request contacts, photos, or precise location for these features.

1.2 Audio and AI processing

Voice input you submit in Home is recognized through AICenter streaming ASR. Short audio you submit in Teaching and Review is recognized through AICenter batch ASR. Related product speech is synthesized by ElevenLabs; that path does not send your microphone audio. Warmup, Experience, and Course Simulation realtime calls are forwarded, after LingoMate authentication, to an ElevenLabs Hosted Agent, which handles voice-activity detection, speech recognition, turn-taking, and streaming speech synthesis on the same call connection. Experience and Course Simulation Voice Sessions record bounded transcripts and whiteboard lesson evidence during the call; the settled final record is written to the Learning Attempt. Warmup Voice Sessions and Learning Attempts keep neither call transcripts nor whiteboard. None of these flows store raw classroom audio. Home audio does not enter chat history or lesson evidence. Long audio files that authorized Admin or Teacher Assist users upload are transcribed by Soniox.

Chat, memory extraction, lesson analysis, and review process necessary bounded text through the shared AICenter model data plane. The app is not allowed to hold model-provider credentials directly.

1.3 Local information

The app stores the random installation identifier, installation credential, current user session, and deletion-recovery state in the device Keychain, and stores learning-profile cache, notification navigation targets, settings, necessary lesson cache, and enabled analytics SDK queues in other local storage. Deleting the app does not necessarily delete Keychain data or server data. You may request deletion under section 5.

3. Storage location, period, and cross-border processing

Application services and operational logs currently use Alibaba Cloud infrastructure in Singapore. Business data is stored in MongoDB configured on the server; the actual database region follows the published configuration and provider records. Model processors routed by AICenter, ElevenLabs, Soniox, Apple, ThinkingData, AppsFlyer, Meta, and Google Firebase may also process necessary information outside your country.

If that processing is a cross-border transfer of personal information, we will meet applicable notice, consent, assessment, certification, standard-contract, or other requirements.

We keep information for the shortest period needed for the service purpose. Chat, lesson records, learning plans, progress, and memory stay while you use the service until you delete them or ask us to. Security logs, job records, and backups stay for security, incident handling, and legal duties. Processor temporary copies are cleared under their contracts and applicable law. Information the law requires us to keep, or that is needed for a dispute, may remain with restricted access.

4. Security

We use HTTPS, app request signatures, user sessions, access control, least privilege, request and response size limits, timeouts, log redaction, idempotent writes, and limited background jobs. Original installation and session credentials stay only in the device Keychain; the server stores hashes. Installation credentials, session credentials, APNs tokens, model credentials, raw attribution responses, raw chat, prompts, audio, and memory bodies must not be written to ordinary operational logs, analytics events, crash fields, or metric labels.

Internet services cannot be guaranteed absolutely secure. If a security incident may affect your rights, we will take remedial measures required by law and notify or report as required.

5. Your rights and choices

  • In the app you can edit English level, learning goals, guidance style, and correction frequency; view the learning plan; change weekly lesson times; add or cancel an individual lesson; and turn lesson reminders on or off. Other corrections can be requested through the contact below.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of relationship memory tied to your user through the contact below. The current app has no relationship-memory switch or per-item manager.
  • You may request deletion of a single Home session, all Companion data, or complete user data through the contact below. The current release build does not include an in-app Restore as New User control. Complete deletion revokes that user’s sessions on every installation and does not affect records the law requires us to keep.
  • You may withdraw microphone, notification, or App Tracking permission in system settings. After tracking permission is withdrawn, related SDKs are no longer allowed to read the advertising identifier.
  • Korean residents may also request access, correction, deletion, suspension of processing, and withdrawal of consent under the Personal Information Protection Act.

Deleting one session, all Companion data, and complete user data are different scopes. Learning Attempt and Voice Session records are held by separate owners; only a complete deletion request coordinates those owners. Complete deletion applies to every installation of the same user, not only the device that asked. We may ask for a user identifier or other information needed to verify the request. We will reply within the period required by law. Deleting server data does not automatically clear legally retained third-party security records or local cache on your device; you may also uninstall the app or clear local data.

6. Children

Users under 18 should read and agree to this policy with a guardian before using the service. We do not collect age information to identify or profile children. If a guardian believes we processed a child’s information inappropriately, contact us to verify and handle it.

For material changes to processing purposes, information types, sharing parties, or cross-border arrangements, we will notice you before the change takes effect through an app page, dialog, notification, or other reasonable method, and we will obtain consent again when the law requires it.
