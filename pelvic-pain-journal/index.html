Before you start

Do I need a diagnosis to use it?

No. You can record your own symptoms without entering a diagnosis. The app is a personal journal and appointment preparation tool; it does not identify the cause of pain.

Is it only for period pain?

No. You can record pelvic pain and related symptoms with or without a cycle. Cycle context includes “Not sure” and “No cycle.”

What does the PDF include?

It summarizes your selected period’s logs, including recorded pain, logged days, symptom and location counts, medication entries, and recent notes. It does not interpret the records or recommend treatment.

Does it work across devices?

This version keeps your journal locally and does not include cloud sync. It includes JSON backup export. For help with your records, see the support page.

What do I pay for?

The App Store purchase includes PDF export, 90-day trends, full local history, custom symptom labels, and JSON backup. There is no subscription or in-app purchase. Check the App Store for current availability, device requirements, and your regional price.
